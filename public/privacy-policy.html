OFFICIAL POLICY

Privacy Policy for CampusOne

Effective Date: October 2026 · Last Updated: October 2026

Overview: CampusOne is an all-in-one university companion app developed for Bangladesh University of Business & Technology (BUBT), Dhaka, Bangladesh. We are committed to safeguarding the privacy and security of students, faculty, and administrative staff who use the application.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the minimum required information necessary to provide campus utilities and peer-to-peer services:

  • Account Details: Your full name, university email address (@std.bubt.edu.bd or official BUBT staff email), and unique Student / Staff ID.
  • Academic Profile: Department, intake number, and section (used exclusively to organize study hub resources, note sharing, and class routine schedules).
  • Voluntary Contact Information: Phone and WhatsApp number provided voluntarily for marketplace item coordination or campus ride-sharing. Phone numbers are hidden by default and only disclosed when a fellow student initiates an authorized contact reveal.
  • User-Generated Content: Photos submitted for lost and found matching, study documents/notes uploaded to the Study Hub, and maintenance report descriptions with optional issue photos.
  • Device Identifiers & Diagnostics: Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) device tokens used solely for push notification delivery (e.g. urgent blood requests, maintenance updates), and anonymized crash diagnostics via Sentry.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used strictly to power university mobile features:

  • To authenticate and maintain your active campus session.
  • To deliver urgent campus blood donation appeals and connect matched volunteer donors.
  • To dispatch campus maintenance issue reports directly to university maintenance crews.
  • To facilitate peer-to-peer lost item recovery and campus route ride-sharing.
  • To answer campus queries through the in-app Gemini AI assistant without retaining user prompts for public AI training.

3. Data Sharing & Third-Party Services

CampusOne does NOT display commercial third-party ads and NEVER sells or rents personal data to third parties.

We work with trusted infrastructure providers to power app capabilities:

  • Supabase: Cloud database, authentication, and encrypted asset storage with Row-Level Security (RLS) enforcement.
  • Google Firebase (FCM): Push notification delivery.
  • Google Gemini: Ephemeral campus chatbot query processing.
  • Sentry: Real-time crash diagnostics and telemetry.

4. Data Security

All data in transit is encrypted using Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3 / HTTPS). Account credentials and sensitive database records are secured using enterprise-grade cryptographic standards. We enforce strict PostgreSQL Row-Level Security (RLS) to ensure users can only access their authorized records.

5. Account & Data Deletion (User Rights)

You have the right to request deletion of your account and all associated personal data at any time:

  • In-App Deletion: Open the app, go to Settings → Account → Delete Account, and confirm deletion. This permanently purges your account, active marketplace listings, ride offers, and messages from our database immediately.
  • Web Deletion: If you have uninstalled the application, you can submit a deletion request via our Account Deletion Request Page or by emailing campusone.bubt@gmail.com with your registered university email.

6. Children's Privacy

CampusOne is intended for university students, faculty, and administrative staff who are at least 18 years of age or enrolled in higher education. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

7. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect campus service updates. Changes become effective immediately upon posting. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
