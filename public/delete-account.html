USER DATA POLICY

CampusOne Account & Data Deletion

In accordance with Google Play Developer Policy and data protection regulations, CampusOne users can permanently delete their account and associated personal data at any time.

Permanent Action: Deleting your account will immediately and permanently erase your user profile, student ID links, active marketplace listings, ride shares, connection requests, and push notification tokens. This action cannot be reversed.

Method 1: Instant In-App Deletion (Recommended)

If you have the CampusOne mobile app installed on your Android device:

  1. Open the CampusOne app.
  2. Tap on the Settings tab at the bottom right.
  3. Scroll down to the Account section.
  4. Tap Delete Account and tap Permanently Delete in the confirmation prompt.

Your data is wiped from the database immediately.

Method 2: Web Deletion Request (App Uninstalled)

If you have uninstalled the app or lost device access, email our engineering team directly from your registered university email address:

Submit Deletion Request via Email

Please include your registered university email and Student ID. Web requests are processed within 24 to 48 business hours.
